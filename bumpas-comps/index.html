How pricing works
Pricing & timing

Price it right and the whole market shows up.

Buyers shop by price. Put your number where the market is, and most of them can see it. Set it high to "leave room," and you screen out the buyers who'd have paid you fairly.

Priced at market, most buyers can see your home. Every step above it loses more of them.

Buyer attention by week on the market

Your best two weeks are the first two. A new listing is fresh to every buyer and every agent. That attention spikes, then fades.

Overpriced homes don't wait politely. They sit, go stale, and end up selling for less than if they'd been priced right on day one.

What moves the number
Value drivers

Why two similar homes sell for different prices.

Same street, same floor plan, different number. It comes down to a handful of things.

01
Location

Buyers pay more to be near schools, parks, and the things they care about.

02
Size

More house and more lot usually means more money.

03
Beds & baths

An extra bedroom or bath can move a home into a whole different buyer pool.

04
Condition

Move-in ready beats dated. Buyers price in the work they'd have to do.

05
Features & finishes

Hardwood, quartz, a real kitchen. Finishes show up in the offer.

06
Curb appeal

The first photo and the first drive-by decide whether buyers step inside.

04 · Inspections
04Before we list, no matter which option

Three inspections up front.

Whichever option you pick, I want three inspections done before the house goes on the market. Home, HVAC, and pest. Buyers who know what needs to be done write clean offers. Buyers who have to guess assume the worst, price it in, or walk after they find out.

Home inspection

Roof, foundation, electrical panel, plumbing, windows, and the additions. On a 1950 house this report answers the questions every buyer is going to ask anyway.

HVAC inspection

The A/C was new in 2017, so it's nine years old. The 2017 listing also showed window units. The inspector will tell us what it has left. HVAC is the first thing buyers at this price use to renegotiate.

Pest inspection

Termites, dry rot, moisture. Most lenders want it anyway. A clear Section 1 list up front avoids the surprise that kills deals on older West Sac houses.

Three reports, done before we list, attached to the disclosures. It costs some money now and saves a renegotiation later.

05 · Open items
05Open items

Three things that move the number.

  • Square footage. The assessor has the house at 1,514 sqft. Some public sources show 1,870 to 1,915. We measure before we list. If there's a discrepancy, we disclose it.
  • Plumbing. Known repair, about $13,000. In options A and B it's disclosed and comes off the price. In C it's your call: fix it or credit it. In D it's done before we list. Either way, buyers know about it before they write.
  • The big systems. Foundation, roof, panel, plumbing, and the additions. Cosmetic work moves the price a little. These move it a lot. The inspections in 04 cover all of it.

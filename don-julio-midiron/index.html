Where the market is right now.

The market is slower than it was in the spring. Prices are holding, but buyers are pickier and sellers are giving more.

What this means for you

  • Most homes that are selling are in your price range. 41% of homes that went pending after rates passed 7% were priced between $400,000 and $600,000.
  • Priced right, homes still sell. 53% of recent pending homes didn't need a price cut. The ones that did cut an average of 6.3%.
  • Sellers are paying more of the buyer's costs. Plan on that when we sell Don Julio, and ask for it when we buy Midiron.
  • Rates went from about 6% in January to over 7% in September. It takes a couple of months for that to show up in sales, so I'll update these numbers before we list and before we make an offer.

What Don Julio is worth.

Three ways to sell it. These numbers assume no major repairs. If it needs a roof, HVAC, or foundation work, they come down.

Sell to an investor as-is
$390,000

No repairs, no cleaning, no showings. A tenant can stay in place. Closes in two to three weeks.

There's no buyer's agent, so you don't pay the 2.5% buyer agent fee.

Based on 4708 Don Julio and 6920 Waxwing, minus the work an investor will do.
Better
List it empty and clean
$435,000

The house is empty, deep cleaned, and the yard is cleaned up. Small repairs only.

Plan on paying $5,000 to $10,000 of the buyer's closing costs.

New paint, new flooring where it's worn, new light fixtures, and staging. No kitchen remodel. We list at $469,900.

Based on 4708 Don Julio ($476K, full remodel) and 6920 Waxwing ($482K, bigger lot).

What Midiron is worth.

What Midiron is worth depends on its condition. Here are three values.

One thing to check before we offer. The MLS lists 1,842 sqft and the county lists 1,579. If the extra 263 sqft isn't permitted, the appraisal will use 1,579. These numbers use 1,579.

$500,000

Original kitchen and baths, older roof and HVAC. You'll be competing with cash investors.

06 · Open items
06Open items

What I need from you.

  • Is there a tenant at Don Julio? An investor can buy with a tenant in place. To list it, the house needs to be empty. Talk to me before anyone gives notice.
  • What's been updated at Don Julio? Roof, HVAC, windows, kitchen, or baths. Updates raise the price.
  • What condition is Midiron in? That decides which price applies. Good to Best is $140,000.
  • Is Midiron's extra square footage permitted? 1,842 sqft or 1,579 changes the appraisal.
  • Taxes on Don Julio. You've owned it since 1986. Talk to your CPA before we list.
